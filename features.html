How the tools work together

A clear starting point

Church registration and verification establish the church space before members request access. Membership approval gives leaders a deliberate way to welcome people into church-specific tools.

Keep communication together

Announcements provide the church’s official updates, while Community gives members a place for testimonies and encouragement. Ministry groups and Grace Rooms serve smaller conversations and team coordination.

Make spiritual habits part of the day

Bible reading, Daily Word, Bible quizzes, and study groups bring reflection and learning into everyday church life. Members can return to these resources between services.

Plan beyond Sunday

Events, RSVPs, calendar reminders, and attendance support the practical side of ministry. Leaders can connect upcoming activities with the people who need to hear about them.

Care with appropriate boundaries

Prayer requests and private pastoral care have different purposes. Church roles and access controls help keep church responsibilities with the people assigned to them. Read the pastoral-care and staff-access policies for the full guidance.

Understand access before subscribing

Community features and paid church-workspace tools have different access rules. The Pricing page explains what an active subscription unlocks and what remains available when church-workspace access is paused.
