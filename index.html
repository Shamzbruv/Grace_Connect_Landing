Monthly billing, confirmed by people

Prices are monthly in USD. Submitting a subscription or quote request does not charge your church or enrol it in a paid plan. Under the current workflow, payment and activation are confirmed with the platform team and subscriptions do not renew automatically.

What a paid workspace unlocks

An active subscription unlocks the member directory, attendance, announcements, ministry and study-group management, private prayer care and counselling, scheduling, roles, church finance and analytics, and live-service management.

What remains available

Without an active subscription, members can still use Community, public events and RSVPs, Bible reading and Daily Word, Grace Rooms, Saved, profiles, notifications, messaging, and church transfer. Only paid church-workspace tools are paused.

Cancellation and trials

An authorised pastor, church administrator, treasurer, financial secretary, or subscription manager can submit a cancellation request in the app and state the desired timing. Access changes when the platform team records the cancellation. Trials end on their recorded date and never convert automatically to a paid plan.
