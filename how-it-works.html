Grace Connect · How It Works

A thoughtful path into your church community

A practical guide for church leaders and members, from registration to everyday fellowship.

Your Journey to a Connected Church

Getting started is simple and secure.

1

Pastor/Admin registers the church

Fill out the registration form with your church's verified details.

2

Church is reviewed and approved

Our team verifies the church to maintain a safe platform.

3

Pastor/Admin receives confirmation

An email is sent when the church is officially active.

4

Members sign up and search

Congregation members search for the church to request access.

5

Members enter their digital space

Once approved by the admin, members enjoy full access.

Know what to expect at each stage

For church leaders

Have your church name, denomination, contact information, address, and leadership details ready. Complete the church application and the required age, authority, and policy confirmations.

Verify your email

Follow the email-verification step presented during registration. Return to the completion step to submit the application. Email verification and church approval are separate parts of the process.

While the church is reviewed

The church application remains pending until it has been reviewed. Wait for the church-approval confirmation before asking members to search for the church in the approved directory.

For congregation members

Open Member Access, search for your approved church, and select it before completing the sign-up form. Verify your email and complete the membership-request step when prompted.

After requesting membership

Church-specific access opens after the church’s pastor or administrator approves the request. The existing Community access rules still apply while a membership request is pending.

Introduce the church’s digital routine

Once access is ready, show members where to find announcements, prayer, events, Bible study, and their ministry groups. Leaders can then introduce the tools relevant to each member’s role.

Take the next step with your church

Start a church application, or find your approved church and request membership.
