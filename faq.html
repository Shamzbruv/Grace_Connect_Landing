Subscriptions, access, and policies

No. Submitting a subscription or quote request does not charge the church or enrol it in a paid plan. Payment and activation are confirmed with the platform team.

Under the current workflow, subscriptions do not renew automatically. Trials end on their recorded date and do not automatically convert to a paid plan.

Only paid church-workspace tools are paused. Community, public events and RSVPs, Bible reading and Daily Word, Grace Rooms, Saved, profiles, notifications, messaging, and church transfer remain available under the existing access rules.
