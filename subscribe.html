What happens next

Three steps, and the last one is automatic.

  1. You pay securely. We send you to our payment provider's page with the amount locked in. It cannot be edited.
  2. We confirm the payment. The provider notifies Grace Connect directly, so confirmation does not depend on your browser staying open.
  3. Your church is activated. Billing details and the paid-through date appear in the app and on the manage page, and the finance team's records update automatically.
